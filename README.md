# Nigeria Food Insecurity Intelligence

Project 02 — Nigeria in Data: Beyond the Numbers.

Independent research by Christian Somtoo Obiechina.

## Predicting Hunger Under Socioeconomic Distribution Shift

A longitudinal, statistical and explainable AI investigation of Nigeria's food insecurity crisis, 2018–2026, with 2027 risk scenarios.

Historical survey analysis covers 9,691 household-wave observations and 4,682 matched households. Reported whole-day hunger increased from 6.39% to 13.04%. The best exploratory longitudinal ROC-AUC was 0.602; cross-wave models performed near chance. These models are not suitable for automated aid eligibility.

The November 2026 Cadre Harmonisé assessment and 2027 seasonal projection are separate population-level evidence, not household model predictions.
